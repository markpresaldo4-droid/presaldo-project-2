Welcome to Kabankalan Catholic College

Kabankalan Catholic College Inc. is an educational institution dedicated to helping students gain knowledge, develop skills, and build good values for their future.

  • Providing quality education and learning opportunities for students.
  • Promoting discipline, respect, honesty, and responsibility.
  • Encouraging students to develop their talents, improve their abilities, and participate in school activities and community service.

Curricular Offerings

Education

Bachelor of Elementary Education (BEEd)

Prepares future teachers to support children's learning and development in elementary education.

Education

Bachelor of Early Childhood Education (BECEd)

Focuses on teaching and supporting the growth and learning of young children.

Education

Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSEd)

Majors: English, Filipino, Mathematics, Science, and Values Education.

Sports and Education

Bachelor of Physical Education (BPEd)

Develops knowledge and skills in physical education, fitness, sports, and movement activities.

Arts and Humanities

Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy (ABPhilo)

Explores philosophical ideas, ethics, reasoning, and critical thinking.

Social Sciences

Bachelor of Science in Psychology (BSPsych)

Studies human behavior, mental processes, and psychological principles.

Criminal Justice

Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BSCrim)

Introduces crime, criminal justice, law enforcement, and public safety.

Business

Bachelor of Science in Accountancy (BSA)

Focuses on accounting principles, financial reporting, auditing, and taxation.

Business

Bachelor of Science in Management Accounting (BSMA)

Develops skills in cost analysis, budgeting, and financial decision-making.

Business Administration

BSBA Major in Financial Management

Covers financial planning, investment, and business finance.

Business Administration

BSBA Major in Marketing Management

Explores marketing strategies, customer relations, promotion, and market research.

Technology

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)

Develops skills in programming, web development, databases, and information systems.

Tourism and Hospitality

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management (BSTM)

Covers tourism operations, travel services, destination management, and customer service.

Supplemental Program

Supplemental Program for Non-Education Bachelor's Degree Graduates

An additional academic pathway for eligible graduates who need professional education preparation.

Professional Education

18 Units Professional Education

Professional education coursework for eligible bachelor's degree graduates.

College Administration

Leadership and Management

Teaching and Learning

Faculty members help students gain knowledge, improve their skills, and develop good values through meaningful learning experiences.
