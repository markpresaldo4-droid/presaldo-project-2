About Us

Kabankalan Catholic College Inc. is committed to providing quality education that helps students develop their knowledge, skills, talents, and good values. Through effective teaching, academic guidance, and meaningful learning experiences, the college encourages students to become responsible, competent, and disciplined individuals who are prepared to face future challenges and contribute positively to their families, communities, and society.

KABANKALAN CATHOLIC COLLEGE INC.

Empowering students through quality education, strong values, academic excellence, and meaningful learning experiences.

Kabankalan Catholic College Inc. aims to provide an educational environment where students can discover their potential, strengthen their abilities, and develop the values necessary for personal and professional growth.

  • Providing quality education and meaningful learning experiences.
  • Developing students' knowledge, skills, talents, and confidence.
  • Promoting discipline, responsibility, respect, and good moral values.
  • Preparing students to become productive and responsible members of society.
